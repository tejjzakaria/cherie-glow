# Chérie Glow — Product Catalog

## About the Brand

**Chérie Glow** is a Moroccan intimate wellness brand specializing in edible flavored body oils — the **Gourmand Collection**. Every product is made from 100% natural ingredients, safe to taste and touch, designed to bring couples closer through shared sensory experiences.

The brand is built around three core values:

- **Naturalness** — all oils are extracted from real fruits, chocolate, and vanilla. No synthetics, no artificial fragrances.
- **Intimacy** — products are meant to be applied to sensitive areas and shared with a partner. The scent and taste create moments of closeness that words can't.
- **Privacy** — all orders are shipped in discreet, unmarked packaging. Cash on delivery only. No prepayment required.

Delivery covers all of Morocco within 24–48 hours.
